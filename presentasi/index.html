04 • Routine

Easy to explain. Easy to follow.

The full routine is designed to feel intuitive for customers and simple for partners to communicate.

01. CleanseStart with Brightening Gentle Cleanser on damp skin, massage gently, then rinse.
02. SerumApply Plump+Bright Serumiser to clean skin and allow it to absorb.
03. MoisturizeUse Lumiere Essence Hydra Cream or Fresh Hydra Cream based on preference and comfort.
04. Body CareApply Radiance Treatment Body Serum evenly after bathing as part of a regular body-care routine.
