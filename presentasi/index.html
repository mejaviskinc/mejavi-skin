Daily Ritual

A simple sequence to explain.

The routine is organized so the audience can understand how the core Mejavi Skin+ products fit together without turning the presentation into a shopping page.

01

Cleanse

Wet the face, apply Brightening Gentle Cleanser, massage gently, rinse thoroughly and pat dry.

02

Serum

Apply Plump+Bright Serumiser to clean skin and allow it to absorb before the moisturizer step.

03

Moisturize

Apply Lumiere Essence Hydra Cream in a thin, even layer to the face and neck as needed.

04

Alternative Cream

Fresh Hydra Cream can be used as an alternative moisturizer according to skin needs and compatibility.

05

Body Care

After bathing, apply Radiance Treatment Body Serum to clean, dry body skin and massage until absorbed.

Results and skin responses can vary. Use each product according to its packaging directions and introduce new products gradually, especially when skin is sensitive.
